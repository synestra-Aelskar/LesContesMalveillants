## Prompt

Create a single square Campement game menu icon that belongs EXACTLY to the same icon family as the two supplied reference icons (books and sliders). References are style only. Subject: a small heraldic campfire, three sculpted flame tongues over two crossed wooden logs, frontal and compact. Render as an engraved antique brass and pale muted gold relief emblem, with dark brown recesses, thin pale gold contour highlights, restrained fine etched details and large nearly black negative spaces. The flame itself is SOLID ENGRAVED METAL, not actual glowing fire. Match the references' limited desaturated brass palette, line thickness, dark background RGB about 15,15,17, visual weight and restrained dimensionality. Emblem occupies central 65-70% of square, centered, balanced and readable at 46px. No tent, no scene, no blazing light, no bloom, no orange, no yellow saturation, no red, no text, no outer frame, no circle, no pedestal. Finished production icon.
