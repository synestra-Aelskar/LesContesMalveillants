## Couverture

Strictly straight front view, closed upright rectangular book, no perspective angle. Genuine transparent background. Book should fill image with only 2 percent transparent margin, portrait aspect ratio 470:650. Proportions of a real readable book, moderate thin ornamental border, broad quiet black leather center. Keep all ornaments confined to outer 12 percent border and top 10 percent. Leave the entire central rectangle x18%-83%, y16%-83% empty clean black leather for dynamic title and text, no central emblem. No lettering, no text, no watermark, no scene, no drop shadow outside book. Refine edges and lighting, avoid excessive noise and overly shiny gold. Keep it recognizably the provided Necronicon, not a generic plain book.
